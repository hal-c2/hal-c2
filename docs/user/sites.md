# Publishing a page as a site

Publish a page from a Space to get a link anyone can open in a browser, with no pairing or
sign-in. Use it to share a page with someone who doesn't use HAL-C2.

## Publish

Open the page and choose **Publish as a site** from its actions. The page then shows that it is
published, with the link and a way to copy it.

The site is a copy of the page at that moment. Later edits stay private, and the page says when
its site is behind. Choose **Publish again** to update the site; the link stays the same.

## Stop publishing

Choose **Unpublish** and the link stops working. Moving the page to the trash or deleting it does
the same, and restoring it from the trash doesn't publish it again. Publishing after any of these
makes a new link.

## Who can open the link

Anyone who has the link and can reach your MC's address. The link is the only protection, so share
it as you would a password. It opens this one page and nothing else on the MC, and comments are
never included.

The link uses the address you reached the MC at. When your MC only listens on this machine, the
page says so: only this machine can open that link. Choose **Publish over Tailscale** to get a link
others on your tailnet can open, or see [Remote access](remote-access.md) to put the MC on your
network.

## What a site shows

Text, headings, checklists and callouts, as a plain page. Links to other pages show as plain text,
and images aren't included.
